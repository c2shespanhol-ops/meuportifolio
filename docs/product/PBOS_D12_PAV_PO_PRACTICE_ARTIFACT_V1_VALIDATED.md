## 1. Governance decision

The D12 artifact is formally validated as a **reconstructed Product Owner practice artifact** based on the user's historical experience in the implementation of a Receita Federal PAV in Maricá.

This document does **not** claim that the user held a formal Product Owner position or that the original project was conducted under a formal Product Management framework.

The distinction between **historical evidence** and **PO-language reconstruction** must remain explicit.

## 2. Validated points

### 01. Origin and scope of participation

The initiative originated from a Prefeitura demand to bring eligible Receita Federal services closer to citizens in Maricá. The user participated from Discovery through implementation and delivery follow-up.

### 02. Discovery activities

The user participated in conversations with citizens, journey mapping, data analysis, meetings with other areas, direct interaction with Receita Federal and requirements definition.

### 03. Existing reference model

Receita Federal already had a PAV model used in other locations. The user did not create the model from scratch and participated in a visit to one existing PAV to observe structure, equipment, services, execution, systems/access, support and training.

### 04. Adaptation to local context

The existing model was analyzed and adapted to the Maricá context. The user participated in implementation across three Maricá units. This should be represented as adaptation and implementation of an existing model, not as creation of the solution from scratch.

### 05. Requirements and readiness

The user participated in defining and checking concrete implementation requirements, including space/layout, computers, printer, scanner, internet, system access, operational conditions, support and training/orientation. A readiness check was used before service operation.

### 06. Role in the solution

The user did not independently own or define the entire solution. The contribution consisted of participating with the involved areas and Receita Federal in requirements, journey, benchmark, adaptation, validation and implementation follow-up.

### 07. Implementation and readiness

The user's participation extended beyond requirements into implementation and operational readiness, including adaptation of the model, preparation of units, system access, equipment, training/orientation and verification of conditions before operation.

### 08. User validation

The user participated in validation with users after implementation, checking whether the service worked in practice and contributing to necessary adjustments.

### 09. Implementation result

The implementation resulted in three PAV units in Maricá, the service entering operation, continued post-launch support and positive institutional recognition from Receita Federal.

No attendance, satisfaction, travel-cost or cost-reduction figures are invented or inferred.

### 10. External validation and learning

A Receita Federal representative/auditor visited the work and evaluated the implementation positively, highlighting that the result was above expectations and ahead of similar initiatives.

The experience demonstrates a practical ability to investigate a real problem, understand a journey, work with stakeholders, translate needs into requirements, adapt an existing solution, support implementation and validate the result.

## 4. Explicit claim boundaries

The following claims remain prohibited unless new evidence is validated:

- formal Product Owner title;
- E2E ownership of the product/service;
- creation of the PAV model from scratch;
- independent definition of the Receita service catalog/Carta de Serviços;
- independent ownership of Product Vision or Product Strategy;
- Product Roadmap ownership;
- formal KPI/analytics ownership;
- three benchmark visits;
- quantitative citizen attendance, satisfaction, cost or travel-reduction results not evidenced.
