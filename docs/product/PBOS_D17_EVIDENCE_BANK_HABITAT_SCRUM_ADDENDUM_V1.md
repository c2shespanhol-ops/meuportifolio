## Purpose

Record a newly discovered historical evidence item from the D16 technical review so it is not lost while the canonical Evidence Bank file is reconciled.

## New historical evidence

During the D16 technical review, the user explicitly confirmed that the Habitat experience included accumulated responsibilities across **Customer Experience (CX), Product Owner and Scrum Master functions**.

The user confirmed practical use of **Scrum with the development team for new functionality**, including recurring meetings on Fridays.

Confirmed activities:

- Product Backlog: worked with backlog items, but final prioritization authority belonged to the product owner/owner of the product;
- Backlog Refinement: helped understand, detail and clarify items before development;
- Sprint Planning: helped the team understand the customer's need and define the work to be performed;
- Daily Scrum: participated and, when conducting it, facilitated the conversation, followed progress, identified impediments and supported their resolution;
- Sprint Review: participated in reviewing and validating developed functionality;
- Sprint Retrospective: participated in identifying process problems and defining improvements for the next cycle;
- Impediment handling: identified and acted to remove or route impediments;
- Functionality validation: evaluated developed functionality against the identified need and returned it for adjustment when it did not meet the expected need.

## Product Owner evidence

The user did **not** hold final authority for prioritizing new functionality. The product owner/owner of the product held that decision.

However, the user sometimes influenced decisions by bringing direct evidence of customer needs when the owner's perception differed from what customers were actually reporting or experiencing.

This supports the competency chain:

**Customer Insight → Evidence → Stakeholder Influence → Product Decision.**

The user also had practical participation in Backlog Refinement and Sprint Planning and autonomy to validate functionality and return it for adjustment when the need was not met.

## Scrum Master evidence

The user accumulated practical Scrum Master responsibilities in the same multifuncional context, especially:

- Daily Scrum facilitation/conduction;
- progress follow-up;
- impediment identification and handling;
- Sprint Retrospective participation;
- process improvement.

This is evidence of practical Scrum Master activity. It does not establish an exclusive Scrum Master role or formal Scrum Master job title.

## CX evidence relationship

The Scrum/Product activities occurred alongside the user's CX responsibilities, including direct contact with customers, understanding needs and validating whether delivered functionality addressed those needs.

This reinforces the user's cross-functional position at the interface between **customer, product/business and development**.

## Claim boundaries

The evidence supports:

- practical Scrum experience;
- Backlog Refinement;
- Sprint Planning;
- Daily Scrum participation and facilitation;
- Sprint Review;
- Sprint Retrospective;
- impediment handling;
- functionality validation;
- customer-evidence-based stakeholder influence;
- multifuncional CX + Product Owner + Scrum Master activity.

The evidence does not support, by itself:

- unilateral backlog prioritization;
- exclusive Product Backlog ownership;
- formal Product Owner job title at Habitat;
- formal Scrum Master job title at Habitat;
- sole ownership of product strategy;
- E2E Product Ownership.
