# PBOS D11 · Habitat PO Practice Artifact v1 · Validated

**Status:** Validated by user  
**Gate:** DG-03  
**Validated:** 2026-09-06

## Validation record

The user validated the ten principal points presented during the incremental validation process.

### Validated points

1. Support occurred primarily through WhatsApp, without a ticketing system or structured knowledge base; existing explanations were generic/incomplete and assumed prior system knowledge.

2. The core problem was user dependency on direct support for recurring questions because available explanations were not sufficiently clear and executable.

3. Habitat had three user types in two product/channel contexts: doctors and room owners used the site; clinic owners used the SaaS. Materials therefore needed to consider persona and journey.

4. A predefined topic list existed, but the user had autonomy to reorder production according to client need, recurrence, impact, difficulty and potential value/autonomy.

5. Larger needs were decomposed into smaller, executable parts, including website registration and SaaS materials.

6. Materials were transformed into step-by-step guidance for users without prior system knowledge, with videos produced as complementary material.

7. Materials were validated by the owner and improved based on persistent doubts, recurrence and usage.

8. Materials were reused for direct user self-service, chatbot knowledge and training, reducing dependence on direct support.

9. Documented outcomes include 100+ materials, 27% fewer recurring demands and 31% lower average resolution time, plus increased autonomy and reuse. These remain documented observed outcomes, not claims of sole causality.

10. The experience may be represented in PO language through problem, persona, need, decomposition, prioritization, backlog, acceptance criteria, validation and continuous improvement, provided the document explicitly distinguishes historical evidence from reconstructed PO practice.

## Governance decision

The D11 artifact is approved as a **validated reconstructed Product Owner practice artifact**.

The distinction between historical evidence and PO-format reconstruction must remain explicit in future versions.
