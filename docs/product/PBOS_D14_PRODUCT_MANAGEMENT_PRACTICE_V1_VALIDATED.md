## Core validated practice

The reconstructed Product Management reasoning follows:

**Problem → User → Outcome → Product Direction → Strategic Choices → Roadmap → Backlog.**

The practice demonstrates reasoning beyond task execution while remaining grounded in previously validated Habitat evidence around recurring user friction, personas, SaaS, Discovery, journey understanding, requirements, decomposition, prioritization, validation, continuous improvement and knowledge reuse.

## Explicit claim boundary

D14 does **not** establish historical ownership of:

- Product Vision;
- Product Strategy;
- formal Product Roadmap;
- formal E2E Product Ownership;
- formal Product KPIs or Product Analytics;
- independent executive product decisions.

Vision, Strategy, Roadmap and strategic sequencing are reconstructed practice elements used to develop Product Management capability.
