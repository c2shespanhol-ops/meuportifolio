**Discovery + Users + Requirements + Prioritization + SaaS + UX/CX + Product Operations + Kanban + practical Scrum exposure**

The positioning remains evidence-led. It does not claim unrestricted end-to-end Product Ownership, formal ownership of Product Vision/Strategy/Roadmap, or formal ownership of every Product Backlog decision.

## 5. Important historical clarification incorporated

Habitat experience includes practical work across CX, Product Owner activities and Scrum Master activities in interaction with the development team for new functionalities.

Confirmed practical Scrum exposure includes Product Backlog work, Backlog Refinement, Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum participation and facilitation, Sprint Review, Sprint Retrospective, impediment handling and validation of developed functionality, including returning functionality for adjustment when the customer need was not met.

The final prioritization of new functionalities remained with the product owner/product owner of the business. Therefore, the PBOS must distinguish practical Product Owner contribution from unilateral backlog ownership.

## 6. Claim boundaries

The following boundaries remain mandatory:

- Do not claim formal Product Owner title unless the specific employment record supports it.
- Do not claim unilateral ownership of Product Backlog prioritization where the evidence says the final decision belonged to the product owner/owner.
- Do not convert reconstructed artifacts into historical company documents.
- Do not claim Product Vision, Product Strategy or Roadmap ownership as historical fact without direct evidence.
- Do not claim E2E Product Ownership without evidence.
- Do not claim formal Scrum Master title solely from practical Scrum facilitation and impediment work.
- Do not invent Product Analytics stack, experiments, KPIs or statistical results.
