# PBOS_D10 · Product Owner Positioning & Development Plan v1

**Status:** Approved
**Gate:** DG-03
**Objective:** Translate validated career evidence into a credible Product Owner positioning and a focused development plan.

## 1. Strategic positioning

The candidate should not be positioned as a Product Owner with full end-to-end product ownership if the evidence does not support that claim.

The strongest defensible positioning is:

> Professional transitioning into Product Owner roles with practical experience in Product/Service Discovery, user needs, personas and journeys, requirements gathering and decomposition, prioritization by value, SaaS, UX/CX, stakeholder management, implementation, validation and Product Operations, supported by strong Kanban knowledge and experience with operational flow.

The positioning should emphasize practical evidence rather than inflate seniority or formal ownership.

The SHV case supports customer journey, onboarding, preventive operational monitoring and Salesforce-based service history.

## 4. Claims that must not be made without additional evidence

Do not state that the candidate:

- formally owned a product end to end;
- defined Product Vision;
- owned Product Strategy;
- owned a Product Roadmap;
- formally managed a Product Backlog as a Product Owner;
- conducted formal Scrum Product Backlog Refinement ceremonies unless independently evidenced;
- created formal User Stories and Acceptance Criteria unless the original artifacts confirm the format;
- owned product KPIs or Product Analytics as a formal product function;
- was the formal Product Owner of Habitat, PAV or SHV initiatives;
- created the PAV solution from scratch;
- defined the Receita Federal Carta de Serviços;
- prioritized the PAV service catalog independently;
- visited three benchmark PAV units.

## 5. Development priorities

### P0 · Evidence Conversion
Convert existing experience into recruiter-readable Product Owner evidence.

Focus:
- problem statement;
- user/persona;
- need/opportunity;
- requirements;
- decomposition;
- prioritization;
- validation;
- measurable outcome where evidence exists.

### P0 · Product Backlog Mechanics
Practice formal PO artifacts using simulated product contexts:
- Epic
- User Story
- Acceptance Criteria
- refinement
- prioritization
- dependencies
- trade-offs

The goal is not to rewrite history, but to acquire fluency and demonstrable practice.

### P1 · Product Management
Develop practical ability in:
- Product Vision
- Product Strategy
- Roadmap
- Outcome-oriented metrics
- Product decision making

### P1 · Product Analytics
Move from operational metrics already experienced toward:
- product behavior;
- adoption;
- activation;
- retention;
- conversion;
- hypotheses;
- experiment interpretation;
- decision making from evidence.

### P2 · Formal Agile/Product Language
Strengthen the translation between existing practical experience and market terminology, without claiming ceremonies or responsibilities that were not actually performed.

## 6. Batman Mode direction

Batman Mode should not restart basic Kanban learning. The candidate already has TKP, KMP, KSD and KSI and practical evidence related to flow and Lead Time.

Batman Mode should concentrate on the identified gaps:

1. Product Backlog
2. User Stories
3. Acceptance Criteria
4. Refinement
5. Product Vision
6. Product Strategy
7. Roadmap
8. Product Metrics
9. Product Analytics
10. PO decision simulations

Each learning block should produce an artifact whenever possible. The artifact should become evidence of demonstrated capability, not merely a study note.

## 7. Market-facing positioning principle

The candidate should compete through the combination of:

**Discovery + Users + Requirements + Prioritization + SaaS + UX/CX + Product Ops + Kanban**

rather than attempting to compete through a claim of seniority or complete Product Management ownership that the evidence does not yet support.
