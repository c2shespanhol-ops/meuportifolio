CASE 01 · MARIA · SUPPORTING DOCUMENTATION

From discovery to experience architecture.

A set of artifacts organizing the design of the Institutional Virtual Assistant for the Tax and Fiscal Management Secretariat of the City of Maricá. The documentation presents the product logic, proposed journey, conversational architecture and requirements defined during Discovery.

Evidence boundary: this case documents solution design and specification. There is no available evidence of production deployment, operational metrics or formal usability testing. The persona was approved by management and used in internal channels; this does not establish that the assistant itself was deployed.

03 · PROPOSED CONVERSATIONAL FLOW

How the experience should respond.

AccessEmployee opens the channel
→
QuestionNatural-language query
→
ClassificationIntent identification
→
Official sourceSearch approved content
→
ResponseClear, traceable guidance
If the answer resolves the question

Confirm understanding and close the interaction.

If uncertainty or complexity remains

Ask for clarification, offer options or route the user to the appropriate human channel.

This conceptual flow is derived from the journey, conversational architecture and requirements artifacts. It does not prove production system behavior.
