CASE 03 · HABITAT CONSULTÓRIOS INTELIGENTES

From individual support to self-service

A B2B SaaS experience combining customer insight, Product practice, UX, Scrum participation and operational improvement.

RESULTS100+ materials created, 27% fewer recurring demands and 31% reduction in average resolution time.
MY CONTRIBUTIONCustomer insight, Discovery, prioritization, requirements, UX, validation and interface with Product and Development.
01 · APPROACH

Turn support knowledge into product value.

DISCOVERY

Start from real behavior

Used support conversations, recurring questions, observed behavior and real cases to identify problems and friction.

PRIORITIZATION

Work by user value

Changed the work order according to user need, recurrence, impact and observed difficulty.

UX & KNOWLEDGE

Design for the persona

Created step-by-step materials and videos that supported users, chatbot content and internal training.

SCRUM PRACTICE

Connect customers and development

Participated in refinement, planning, daily follow-up, review and retrospective, helping the team understand and validate the customer problem.

02 · PRODUCT PRACTICE

Evidence before assumptions.

User Stories & acceptance criteria

Helped clarify customer needs and make the work executable before development.

UAT/OAT

Validated whether delivered functionality and content actually addressed the originating need.

Experimentation

Used tests and observed behavior to improve content and the user experience.

Stakeholder influence

Brought concrete customer evidence into product discussions when perceptions differed.

Ownership boundary
Final product priority remained with the product owner. This case is not presented as formal ownership of the entire Product Backlog or Product Strategy.
