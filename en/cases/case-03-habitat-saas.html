CASE STUDY 03 · HABITAT CONSULTÓRIOS · B2B SAAS

From individual support to self-service

A B2B SaaS experience combining customer insight, product practice, UX, Scrum participation and operational improvement.

REPORTED RESULTS27% fewer recurring demands · 31% operational efficiency increase
ContextB2B SaaS
ContributionProduct | CX | UX
PracticeBacklog | Refinement | UAT/OAT
FocusCustomer autonomy and continuous improvement
ROLE & OWNERSHIP

What I did and what this case proves.

My contribution: I connected customer evidence, Product, Operations and Development; worked with backlog refinement, hypotheses, User Stories, acceptance criteria, UAT/OAT, A/B tests and knowledge content. Limit: final ownership of product strategy and priority decisions remained with the product owner.

01 · THE PROBLEM

The operation was answering the same questions again and again.

Recurring questions created dependency

Customer questions and operational requests revealed recurring friction in onboarding, support and product usage.

Turn repeated support into knowledge

The opportunity was to transform recurring answers into structured content supporting users, the chatbot and internal training.

02 · PRODUCT PRACTICE

From customer insight to delivery.

Discovery

Observed customer behavior, recurring questions, difficulties and real cases before defending a solution.

Backlog

Worked with the demand and functionality list and helped structure work for development.

Refinement

Helped detail and clarify work before development, reducing ambiguity about customer needs.

Scrum practice

Participated in Planning, Daily Scrum, Review and Retrospective in a Scrum context.

Validation

Checked whether delivered functionality addressed the originating need and requested adjustments when necessary.

Evidence

Brought concrete customer examples into product discussions when observed needs differed from initial assumptions.

03 · SELF-SERVICE

One solution serving three needs.

100+ knowledge materials

Structured articles, FAQs and supporting videos around recurring user needs and profiles.

SaaS and chatbot

Content was integrated into the SaaS experience and used to support chatbot responses.

Training and autonomy

The same content supported internal training and helped users resolve issues with less direct support.

Reported impact

27% reduction in recurring demands and 31% increase in operational efficiency.

04 · EVIDENCE-BASED DECISIONS

Customer evidence before assumption.

Customer insights

Conversations, cases, recurring questions and observed behavior informed problem understanding.

Evidence

When customer evidence differed from an initial assumption, I brought concrete examples into the discussion.

Value

Recurrence, impact, user need, difficulty and autonomy helped distinguish relevant problems from requests that only appeared urgent.

Influence

My role was to put customer evidence into the conversation so decisions considered the problem observed at the point of use.

Evidence boundary: this experience demonstrates Customer Insights, Problem Discovery, value-oriented prioritization, backlog practice, Scrum participation and evidence-based stakeholder influence. It does not claim sole ownership of Product Backlog, final prioritization or product strategy.
