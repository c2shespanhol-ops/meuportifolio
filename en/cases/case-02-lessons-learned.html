CASE 02 · DOCUMENT 08 · LESSONS LEARNED

Lessons Learned

English version of the original supporting document. The content preserves the scope, reported outcomes and evidence limitations of the source.

01 · Culture

The greatest challenge was cultural change

Technology helped structure the workflow, but the central challenge was changing behaviors. Employees began to trust a centralized journey, TIC gained an organized point of contact and Planning and Modernization took responsibility for curation.

02 · Ownership

Flow problems need an owner

  • Active monitoring: a follow-up mechanism made it possible to act before a request stalled.
  • Do not confuse response with resolution: employee validation showed that a technical response does not guarantee the need was met.
  • Curation improves quality: guiding employees before execution reduces ambiguity and rework.
  • Governance protects the process: clear criteria help balance speed, security and experience.
03 · Security

Governance is also protection

Access reviews showed that process improvement can also strengthen security. Instead of mirroring existing profiles, the approach considered the work actually performed by each employee.

04 · Final insight

What the work reinforced

Efficient processes do not begin with technology. They begin with understanding people's needs and building governance that can sustain decisions over time.

This case reinforced a Product Ownership perspective based on problem understanding, context, journey, decision-making, follow-up and outcomes. Improvement came from combining people, process, technology and governance, not from a tool alone.
