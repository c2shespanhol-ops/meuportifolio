Governance and Prioritization Framework

English version of the original supporting document. The content preserves the scope, reported outcomes and evidence limitations of the source.

01 · Governance cycle

Five connected stages

  1. 01 · GovernanceEstablish responsibility and operating rules.
  2. 02 · PrioritizationAssess criticality and operational impact.
  3. 03 · MonitoringTrack deadlines and ticket movement.
  4. 04 · EscalationAct on deviations and blocked work.
  5. 05 · ImprovementRecord learning and improve the process.
02 · Decision criteria

Principles used to decide

  • Criticality: impact of unavailability or delay on the employee's work.
  • Deadline: reference to the Service Charter and internal monitoring point.
  • Operational impact: priority for requests affecting essential Secretariat activities.
  • Dependency: identify when the employee depends on technical action to complete work.
  • Security: grant access according to job needs, avoiding unnecessary permissions.
  • Experience: validate the solution with the employee before closure.
03 · Control loop

Governance as a continuous cycle

The framework did not end when a request was routed to TIC. Governance followed the request through validation, identified deviations, contacted the responsible area, recorded learning and fed improvements back into the process.
