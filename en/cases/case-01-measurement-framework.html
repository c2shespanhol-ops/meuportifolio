CASE 01 · MARIA · DISCOVERY ARTIFACT

Proposed Measurement Framework

Proposed indicators for evaluating a potential deployment of the Institutional Virtual Assistant, connecting guidance quality, employee autonomy and the need for human support.

Status: framework proposed for a future pilot and validation stage. This case does not establish that the assistant was deployed in production. There are no historical metrics for service time, adoption, self-service resolution or satisfaction to report.

01 · Measurement objective

Define how a future evaluation could determine whether the solution reduces the effort required to find official guidance without compromising accuracy, information security or continuity of service.

02 · Proposed indicators

DimensionSuggested indicatorInterpretation
Information accessTime required to locate guidance supported by an official sourceCompare the current-process baseline with pilot results using equivalent tasks.
AutonomyShare of queries completed without human handoffCount only cases where the guidance was confirmed as sufficient, not merely conversations that ended.
QualityShare of answers aligned with the official source in a reviewed sampleRequire content-owner review and record errors, omissions and source versions.
HandoffShare of queries routed to a human channelSeparate appropriate scope-based handoffs from handoffs caused by poor intent recognition.
ExperienceEmployee rating after the interactionUse a short optional question and record the period, sample and response rate.
Recurring demandChange in the volume of repeated questions by topicCompare equivalent periods and account for seasonality, process changes and internal campaigns.

03 · Minimum requirements for evaluation

  • Define a baseline before the pilot and document the period, population and query volume.
  • Separate normative questions, procedural guidance and requests requiring human handoff.
  • Record the official source used and the version of the content consulted.
  • Review answer samples against accuracy, completeness and source-alignment criteria.
  • Do not treat the end of a conversation as proof of resolution or autonomy.
  • Publish results with denominators, period and sample limitations.
Evidence boundary: the indicators on this page are recommendations for future evaluation. They are not approved targets, measured results, user-testing evidence or proof that the assistant was deployed.
