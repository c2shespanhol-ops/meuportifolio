This reference model organizes observed questions, friction and needs, groups themes and details requests. It is not a historical record of a formal Product Discovery process at Habitat.

02 · Purpose and context

Purpose

Organize signals available from customer service and operations, group recurring questions, clarify needs and turn sufficiently understood topics into requirements for backlog discussion. The model represents a way to connect customer-service reports and questions with product and development conversations.

03 · Scope

What it covers

  • Review recurring questions and friction observed in WhatsApp support.
  • Organize available reports and needs without assuming formal interviews.
  • Detail themes considering need, recurrence, impact, difficulty and potential for self-service.
  • Prepare topics for backlog discussion. Final prioritization of new features remained with the product owner.

What it does not establish

  • Formal research with structured interviews, which is not documented in the source matrix.
  • Controlled experiments or quantitative hypothesis validation.
  • Unilateral authority over final prioritization of new features.
04 · Reference process

From operational signal to requirement

  1. 01Record questions and friction observed in support.
  2. 02Group reports by theme and recurrence.
  3. 03Clarify the need using the information available.
  4. 04Detail the topic, guidance or requirement.
  5. 05Discuss routing to the backlog; the product owner makes the final decision.
