Conceptual Prototype

Initial visual representation and layout simulation for the proposed product interactions.

Document status: Retrospective documentation based on the available case materials. Language: English.

01 · Interface concept

The low-fidelity concept represents a conversational interface focused on readability, quick-access buttons for frequently searched topics and immediate visual feedback during employee interactions.

02 · Hypotheses to validate

The representation organizes visual hierarchy, quick access and the expected interaction feedback. These are design hypotheses for a future validation stage, not measured usability outcomes.

03 · Evidence note

The prototype is conceptual. No documented evidence is available of formal usability testing, user validation of the interface or measurement of information retrieval time.
