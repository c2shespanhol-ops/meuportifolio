Functional Requirements

Expected capabilities identified for the product's first version.

Document status: Retrospective documentation based on the available case materials. Language: English.

01 · Natural-language processing

The system is expected to interpret questions submitted in natural language by employees, identify their intents and map them to the relevant institutional guidance.

02 · Controlled knowledge base

The assistant is expected to search approved normative repositories exclusively, preventing the generation of content without prior validation.

03 · Human support routing

The system is expected to provide an effective handoff to human support teams whenever complexity exceeds the automated scope.

04 · Evidence note

These items describe expected requirements and capabilities. They must not be interpreted as features proven to have been implemented in production.
