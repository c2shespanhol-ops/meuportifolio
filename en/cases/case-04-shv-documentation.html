ARTIFACT 01

Customer journey map

Onboarding→Weekly monitoring→Preventive / emergency action→Salesforce + customer validation→First five deliveries→Automatic supply handoff

The journey highlights the assisted period and the point at which the customer moved to the regular automatic flow.

ARTIFACT 02

Preventive monitoring decision flow

Weekly customer contact→Check tank level→Above 35%: continue monitoring→At / below 35%: assess replenishment→Request Logistics action when needed→Advise Service Center contact

The 35% threshold is presented as a preventive reference, not as an automated trigger or guaranteed delivery SLA.

ARTIFACT 03

Salesforce validation and feedback loop

Emergency replenishment requested→Protocol tracked in Salesforce→Next weekly follow-up→Customer confirms what happened→Compare system record and experience→Close validation loop

The model documents the described confirmation practice. It does not imply a technical integration or automated reconciliation.
