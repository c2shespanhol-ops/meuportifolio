03 · Purpose and context

Purpose of the proposed model

The model aims to measure and optimize the elapsed time between the initial request and its actual delivery to the end user. Where reliable data exists, it can support analysis of predictability, throughput and delivery flow.

It was structured as a reference for diagnosing time-related bottlenecks and supporting capacity decisions using Kanban flow metrics.

04 · Scope

What the model covers

  • Lead Time and Cycle Time by workflow.
  • Identification of queues and bottlenecks.
  • Analysis of delivery capacity by cycle, Sprint or Kanban flow.

Out of scope

  • Individual employee performance evaluation.
  • Long-term financial budget projections.
  • External marketing traffic metrics.

06 · Potential applications

Use only when consistent data is available

  • Monitor workflow where reliable data exists.
  • Support flow analysis when a dependable history is available.
  • Identify waiting points in the workflow.
  • Assess WIP limits if a formal Kanban flow is in place.

These are potential applications, not historical results attributed to Habitat.

07 · Related artifacts

Connected operational references

This report is related to the Operational Protocol, Incident Management, Knowledge Base, Discovery Flow, Operational Workflow, User Journey and Onboarding Model. The existence of these references does not establish that every proposed metric was historically tracked.

08 · Evidence boundary

What can be stated

The main case reports 27% fewer recurring requests and a 31% reduction in average resolution time. No validated historical series supports claims about lead time, predictability or WIP for Habitat. This document therefore presents a measurement model rather than retrospective performance data.
