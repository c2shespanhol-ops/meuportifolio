CASE 03 · FEDERAL REVENUE PAV · REFERENCE ARTIFACT

Requirements and operational readiness

A reference checklist organizing the physical, technological and operational conditions required before service starts.

Evidence boundary: this is a reconstructed reference model based on the validated experience. It is not a historical approved document, original checklist, formal process record or proof that every step occurred in this exact sequence.
01 · IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

Facilities

Available space, layout, service conditions and unit organization.

Equipment

Computers, printer, scanner and peripherals required for execution.

Connectivity and systems

Internet, access credentials, permissions and system availability.

People readiness

Guidance, training and clarity about the service flow and support channels.

Operations

Operating conditions, routing and support for the units.

External dependencies

Alignment with Federal Revenue and the municipal areas involved.

02 · READINESS GATE

Confirm facilities and equipment

Check the availability and working condition of physical resources.

Confirm access and connectivity

Validate internet, systems and required permissions.

Confirm operational guidance

Check whether the people involved understand the flow and support channels.

Verify start conditions

Record pending items and determine whether the unit is ready to start.

These categories are grounded in the documented experience. This is not a literal reproduction of the historical checklist.
