Bringing a public service closer to citizens

Participation in Discovery, requirements, stakeholder alignment and local implementation of an existing Federal Revenue service model.

SCALEImplementation across 3 units in Maricá: Centro, Itaipuaçu and São José.
MY CONTRIBUTIONDiscovery, journey mapping, requirements, Federal Revenue alignment, benchmarking, implementation and validation.
01 · APPROACH

Adapt an existing service to a local context.

DISCOVERY

Understand the citizen journey

Participated in conversations with citizens and journey mapping to understand the need for local access.

STAKEHOLDERS

Align two organizations

Worked with the municipality and Federal Revenue to understand the existing PAV model and implementation conditions.

REQUIREMENTS

Translate needs into implementation requirements

Contributed to requirements covering physical structure, equipment, system access, training and operation.

BENCHMARK

Learn from an existing implementation

Visited an existing PAV and used its structure, equipment, layout, service execution and training as references for the local implementation.
