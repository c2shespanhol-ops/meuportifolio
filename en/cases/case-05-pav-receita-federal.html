CASE STUDY 05 · FEDERAL REVENUE VIRTUAL SERVICE POINTS

Bringing the service closer to citizens without starting from zero.

Participation in Discovery, requirements, stakeholder alignment and local implementation of the Federal Revenue Service's existing Virtual Service Point model in three Maricá locations.

SCALE3 units implemented
ContextCity of Maricá · Tax and Fiscal Management
ContributionDiscovery · Service Design · Product Operations
StakeholdersCitizens · City · Federal Revenue
FocusJourney, requirements and implementation

My role: I participated in Discovery, journey mapping, requirements definition, alignment with the Federal Revenue, benchmarking, implementation and validation. Limit: the PAV model and service structure already existed at the Federal Revenue; my contribution was local, not independent creation of the service.

01 · THE PROBLEM

A public service was far from the citizen.

Access

For some issues, citizens in Maricá needed to travel to Federal Revenue units outside the municipality, making in-person service harder and more costly.

Opportunity

The city could bring part of these services closer through the Federal Revenue's Virtual Service Point model.

Constraint

The Federal Revenue already had a PAV model and service structure used elsewhere. The challenge was local understanding and implementation.

Dependencies

Implementation required alignment between the City and Federal Revenue, plus physical, technological, operational and service requirements.

02 · DISCOVERY

From citizen need to implementation requirements.

Understand the problem

Contributed to understanding the need to bring service closer and the difficulties faced by people accessing Federal Revenue services.

Map the journey

Participated in journey mapping to understand how citizens reached the need and where local service could reduce friction.

Align

Participated in discussions with the Federal Revenue to understand the existing model, implementation conditions and local operation.

Define requirements

Contributed to requirements covering physical structure, equipment, system access, training and operation.

Important limit: I did not define or prioritize the Federal Revenue service catalog and did not define the solution alone.

03 · BENCHMARK & IMPLEMENTATION

Learn from an existing model, then adapt locally.

Benchmark

Visited an existing PAV to observe equipment, layout, service types, support, systems access and training.

Local adaptation

Used the observed implementation as a reference while respecting the Federal Revenue model and local conditions.

Three units

Implementation covered the municipal integrated service units in Centro, Itaipuaçu and São José.

Validation

Participated in delivery follow-up, user validation and operational support after implementation.

04 · RESULTS & EVIDENCE

A local implementation with three units.

3 units

Centro, Itaipuaçu and São José.

1 benchmark

An existing PAV was visited to guide local implementation.

20+ services

Quantity publicly disclosed by the City for Maricá's PAV.

External validation

The experience records positive feedback from a Federal Revenue auditor regarding the implementation.

I do not present service-volume, satisfaction or travel-reduction metrics because those data were not under my management and are not available in the materials used for this case.

05 · WHAT THIS EXPERIENCE DEMONSTRATES

Discovery, service design and stakeholder management.

Service Discovery

Investigate the real problem and journey before execution.

Service Design

Observe citizen touchpoints and the conditions required to deliver the service.

Requirements Gathering

Translate identified needs into implementation and operating requirements.

Stakeholder Management

Work across City, Federal Revenue and citizen perspectives.

Product Operations

Connect requirements, implementation, validation and post-delivery support.

Benchmarking

Use an existing implementation as a reference for a new local implementation.

My contribution was not to create the PAV. It was to participate in the Discovery, requirements, alignment, benchmark, implementation and local validation that brought an existing Federal Revenue model to three Maricá units.
