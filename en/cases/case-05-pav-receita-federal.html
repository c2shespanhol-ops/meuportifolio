Case 05 · Federal Revenue PAV · Maricá

Bring the service closer to citizens, without starting from scratch

Participated in Discovery, requirements definition, alignment with the Federal Revenue and implementation of Virtual Service Points in three areas of Maricá.

About 3 min read

Verifiable result
3 units implemented: Centro, Itaipuaçu and São José
Context and period
Maricá City Hall · SEGET · January 2025 to May 2026
Contribution
Discovery, service design, requirements and stakeholders
Benchmark
1 existing PAV unit visited
External validation
Positive qualitative feedback, as reported by a Federal Revenue auditor

My role: I participated in Discovery, journey mapping, requirements definition, alignment with the Federal Revenue, benchmarking, implementation and local validation. Evidence boundary: the PAV model and service structure already existed at the Federal Revenue. My contribution was local, not the independent creation of the service. I did not define or prioritize the Service Catalog. I do not report service volume, satisfaction or avoided-travel figures because those data were not under my management.

01 · The problem

The service was far from citizens

Distance to service

To resolve certain issues, Maricá residents had to travel to Federal Revenue units outside the municipality.

Local opportunity

The City's request created an opportunity to bring some of these services closer through the PAV model.

Not starting from scratch

The Federal Revenue already operated this model in other municipalities. The challenge was to understand the local problem and make implementation possible.

Inter-organizational dependencies

The solution required alignment between the City and the Federal Revenue, as well as physical, technological, operational and service requirements.

02 · From problem to implementation

How the work was carried out

  1. Understand the problemI participated in conversations with citizens to understand the need to bring the service closer.
  2. Map the journeyI participated in analyzing how citizens reached the point of needing the service and where local assistance could make access easier.
  3. Alinhar com a Receita FederalI participated in discussions to understand the existing model, implementation conditions and operations in Maricá.
  4. Define requirementsI helped translate needs into requirements for physical facilities, equipment, system access, training and operations.
  5. Fazer benchmarkI visited an existing PAV to observe equipment, layout, services, customer service, support, systems and training.
  6. Implementation and follow-upImplementation took place at the Municipal Integrated Service Centers in Centro, Itaipuaçu and São José. I followed delivery, user validation and subsequent support.
03 · How the service works

In-person service delivered by a partner organization

According to Federal Revenue documentation, a PAV is an in-person channel operated by a partner organization, providing guidance, triage and digital forwarding of requests.

In-person service

A PAV is a partner unit, not a Federal Revenue agency.

Triage and guidance

The team reviews the request, guides the citizen and checks whether a digital channel is available.

Digital forwarding

Documents and requests are forwarded digitally to the Federal Revenue teams responsible for analysis.

Support

The model provides support channels, periodic meetings and technical visits.
