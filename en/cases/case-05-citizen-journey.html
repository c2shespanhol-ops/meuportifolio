CASE 05 · FEDERAL REVENUE PAV · REFERENCE ARTIFACT

Citizen journey and Service Discovery

A reference model connecting citizen needs, adaptation of the existing service and local validation.

Evidence boundary: this is a reconstructed reference model based on the validated experience. It is not a historical approved document, original checklist, formal process record or proof that every step occurred in this exact sequence.
01 · REFERENCE SEQUENCE

From need to local operation

Understand the need

Citizen conversations and investigation of the difficulty of accessing Federal Revenue services outside the municipality.

Map the journey

Identify stages, touchpoints and access friction related to in-person service.

Learn from the existing model

Observe a reference PAV unit, including facilities, equipment, services, systems, support and training.

Adapt requirements locally

Translate the observed conditions into local needs for facilities, technology, access and operations.

Follow readiness and implementation

Check the conditions required to start service and follow implementation across three units.

Validate and support

Participate in user validation and post-launch operational support.

The contribution was not to create a service from scratch. It was to understand a local need, learn from an existing model and participate in its adaptation and implementation with the stakeholders involved.
