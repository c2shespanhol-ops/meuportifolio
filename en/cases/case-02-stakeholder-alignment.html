02 · Roles

Who did what

  • Planning and Modernization: process modernization and request curation.
  • SEGET: client area and beneficiary of improved predictability and service.
  • TIC: technical execution partner.
  • Journey focal point: connects employee and TIC through curation, guidance, follow-up, escalation, validation and process improvement.
03 · Alignment

Governance depended on connection

The main change was a clear communication layer. Employees no longer needed to negotiate directly with TIC, and TIC no longer needed to contact each employee individually. Curation took responsibility for the journey and communication quality.
