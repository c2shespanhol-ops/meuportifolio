User Journey

Proposed interaction stages and touchpoints between the employee and the assistant.

Document status: Retrospective documentation based on the available case materials. Language: English.

01 · Discovery and access

During the workday, an employee identifies an operational question and accesses the virtual assistant channel integrated into the Secretariat's internal platform.

02 · Query and resolution

The user submits a question in natural language. The assistant is expected to identify the intent, retrieve the relevant official guideline and present a direct answer with step-by-step instructions.

03 · Validation and feedback

After the response, the assistant checks whether the guidance was understood or whether the employee needs additional support from a specialized human team.

04 · Evidence note

This journey represents the experience proposed during product conception. It does not prove a production workflow or formal user validation.
