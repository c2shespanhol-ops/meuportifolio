Case 04 · SHV Energy

Onboarding and prevention of LPG supply disruption

An operational customer journey focused on the first deliveries: guidance, preventive monitoring, logistics coordination, Salesforce validation and consumption history before the transition to automatic supply.

About 2 min read

Process logic
The first five deliveries build the history that supports automatic supply
Context and period
SHV Energy · large-scale operations · January 2018 to June 2023
System
Salesforce
Preventive threshold
35% tank safety threshold
Emergency window
Up to 48 business hours

My role: I managed onboarding, weekly customer contact, logistics coordination when risks emerged, Salesforce confirmation and the end of individual follow-up. Evidence boundary: this case documents operational practices, not a quantified product KPI. The work remains within the actual scope of Operations and Process Improvement. These capabilities are transferable to Product, but do not amount to formal ownership of Product Strategy, Product Backlog or Product Analytics.

01 · The operational challenge

The risk did not begin when the gas ran out

Safety threshold

Weekly follow-up helped identify in advance when the tank was approaching the 35% threshold.

Emergency replenishment

Emergency replenishment could take up to 48 business hours and depended on operational availability.

02 · Reconstructed journey

Six steps, from onboarding to handoff

  1. OnboardingIntroduction to the company, service, consumption and safety threshold. A three-question satisfaction survey covered service, installation and supply.
  2. Monitoramento semanalDuring the first deliveries, weekly contact informed the customer about tank levels. The 35% threshold served as a preventive margin.
  3. Emergency actionWhen necessary, emergency replenishment was requested from Logistics. The customer was also advised to contact the Service Center if the tank reached the threshold before the next follow-up.
  4. Validation and feedbackThe Salesforce protocol showed whether replenishment had been recorded. During the next contact, I confirmed the event with the customer and compared the record with what had actually happened.
  5. Consumption history and patternsThe first five deliveries built the history used to calculate average consumption and support replenishment intervals.
  6. HandoffThe customer then moved to automatic supply. Individual follow-up ended, and new needs were handled by the Service Center.
03 · What this demonstrates

Capabilities transferable to Product

End-to-end journey

Understanding the lifecycle, from assisted onboarding to transition into the automatic flow.

Risk-based decision-making

Using operational signals and the preventive threshold to anticipate supply disruption.

Closed feedback loop

Comparing Salesforce records with customer confirmation.

Data-informed decisions

Using the first five deliveries to calculate average consumption.

Process limitation: some customers did run out of gas, either because they did not follow the guidance or because a team was unavailable on the requested date.
