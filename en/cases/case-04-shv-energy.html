CASE 04 · SHV ENERGY · CUSTOMER ONBOARDING · OPERATIONS

Preventing supply disruption through customer journey management.

An operational customer journey built around the first LPG deliveries, combining onboarding, preventive monitoring, logistics coordination, Salesforce validation and consumption history before the transition to automatic supply.

OPERATIONAL REFERENCE35% preventive safety threshold · Emergency replenishment could take up to 48 business hours
COMPANYSHV Energy
PERIODJanuary 2018 · June 2023
SYSTEMSalesforce
CONTEXTCustomer onboarding and LPG supply operations
01 · OPERATIONAL CHALLENGE

Act before the customer runs out of supply.

Weekly follow-up aimed to identify when the tank approached the 35% safety threshold. Emergency replenishment could take up to 48 business hours and depended on operational availability.

02 · RECONSTRUCTED CUSTOMER JOURNEY

From assisted onboarding to automatic supply.

01 · Onboarding

Company and service introduction, guidance on consumption and safety threshold, and a three-question survey about service, installation and supply experience.

02 · Weekly monitoring

During the first supply cycles, contacted the customer weekly to check tank level and identify risk before disruption.

03 · Preventive or emergency action

When needed, requested emergency replenishment from Logistics and advised the customer to contact the Service Center.

04 · Validation and feedback loop

Tracked the replenishment protocol in Salesforce and confirmed the event directly with the customer during the next follow-up.

05 · Consumption history

The first five deliveries formed the history used to calculate average consumption and support future replenishment intervals.

06 · Handoff to automatic supply

After the initial period, the customer moved to automatic supply and future needs were directed to the Service Center.
