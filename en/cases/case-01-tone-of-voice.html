Tone of Voice Guide

Institutional communication standards focused on clarity, respect and helpfulness.

Document status: Retrospective documentation based on the available case materials. Language: English.

01 · Communication principles

Objective: lead with the main information and avoid unnecessary wording. Clear: use common words, direct sentences and organized instructions. Respectful: remain professional, courteous and helpful without blaming users for questions or mistakes. Responsible: do not communicate certainty when the source does not support the answer.

02 · Message structure

1. Acknowledge the question without repeating it in full. 2. Present the essential guidance first, supported by institutional content. 3. Organize procedural steps in a logical sequence. 4. Explain limitations transparently when guidance cannot be confirmed. 5. Indicate the appropriate human channel when additional analysis is required.

03 · Examples of the intended tone

Avoid excessive formality. Prefer direct instructions that explain what to check and where to seek help. Avoid unsupported certainty about deadlines or outcomes. When a deadline cannot be confirmed from the reference material, say so and direct the employee to the responsible channel.

04 · Evidence note

This guide records proposed communication principles. It does not prove implementation in a deployed assistant, user testing or measurement of comprehension and satisfaction.
