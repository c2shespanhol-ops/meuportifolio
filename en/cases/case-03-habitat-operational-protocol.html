This document sets out reference guidelines for structuring and standardizing operational workflows at Habitat Consultórios Inteligentes. It was reconstructed in November 2026 from retrospective evidence and is not presented as a protocol formally approved or implemented at Habitat in 2024.

02 · Purpose and context

Purpose

Standardize the handling of operational requests and align delivery expectations across customer service, technical support and product development. The source document frames this need in the context of a growing customer base and dispersed internal communication channels.

03 · Scope

What the protocol covers

  • Level 1 and Level 2 request triage.
  • Criticality classification.
  • Routing relevant needs into the Product Backlog.
  • Execution, escalation, validation and closure.

It does not establish software architecture decisions, customer commercial contracts or external network infrastructure support.

04 · Operating flow

From intake to closure

  1. IntakeRegister the request and its context.
  2. Operational triageUnderstand the issue and classify its criticality.
  3. RoutingDirect the request to the appropriate operational or product owner.
  4. Execution and escalationHandle the request or route dependencies to the responsible team.
  5. ValidationAsk the user to test the outcome when applicable.
  6. ClosureClose the request after the outcome is checked.
05 · Indicators and evidence

Documented outcomes

The quantitative outcomes documented in the main case are a 27% reduction in recurring requests and a 31% reduction in average resolution time. These are reported results; this reconstructed protocol does not contain an auditable time series by period or establish exclusive causality.

Potential applications listed in the source include more consistent operational guidance, greater sprint-planning predictability, lower average handling time and an auditable corporate standard. These are potential benefits, not verified historical outcomes.
