CASE 03 · HABITAT CONSULTÓRIOS INTELIGENTES

Evidence matrix and interpretation boundaries

Traceability document separating validated retrospective facts, reported outcomes and artifacts reconstructed to demonstrate Product reasoning.

Reading rule: the workflow, Discovery, onboarding and incident documents in this case are reference models reconstructed in November 2026. They organize practices and learnings, but do not prove that Habitat operated with these formal processes, SLAs, indicators or governance.

1. Validated historical evidence

Validated fact

Channel and context

Support took place mainly through WhatsApp, without a ticketing system or structured knowledge base. Existing explanations were generic and assumed prior knowledge of the system.

Validated fact

Audiences and journeys

Three profiles: physicians and room owners used the website; clinic owners used the SaaS. Materials needed to account for differences in persona, context and journey.

Validated fact

Content prioritization

A predefined list of topics existed. Cleyton could reorganize the production order based on customer need, recurrence, impact, difficulty and potential for user autonomy.

Validated fact

Decomposition and guidance

Larger needs were divided into executable parts. Materials included step-by-step instructions and, when applicable, complementary videos.

Validated fact

Validation and reuse

Materials were reviewed by the owner and improved based on persistent questions, recurrence and use. Content was reused in self-service, the chatbot and training.

Validated fact

Scrum in practice

Participation in Product Backlog, Backlog Refinement, Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review and Sprint Retrospective. On some occasions, Cleyton facilitated the Daily, followed impediments and supported their resolution.
