CASE 05 · FEDERAL REVENUE PAV · REFERENCE ARTIFACT

User validation plan

A reconstructed model for organizing operational validation after implementation, without claiming a formal historical protocol.

Evidence boundary: this is a reconstructed reference model based on the validated experience. It is not a historical approved document, original checklist, formal process record or proof that every step occurred in this exact sequence.
01 · OBJECTIVE
Check whether the implemented service works in practice for citizens and whether the conditions for access, service delivery and routing are available at the units.
02 · VALIDATION DIMENSIONS

Service access

Can citizens reach the unit and understand how to start?

Guidance

Are initial information and next steps clear?

Operational conditions

Do facilities, equipment, connectivity and access support the flow?

Execution and routing

Does the service follow the expected flow and route the request correctly?

Citizen experience

Are there recurring doubts, barriers or difficulties during use?

Post-launch support

Do identified issues have a channel for routing and follow-up?

03 · LEARNING LOOP

Observe actual operation

Follow service use without assuming adoption or satisfaction.

Record friction

Document questions, access failures, limitations and interruptions.

Align adjustments

Share findings with responsible areas and follow the resulting actions.

Confirm corrections

Check whether the operation now works according to the observed need.

No sample size, success rate, satisfaction score or quantitative outcome is associated with this reconstructed model.
