Case 01 · MarIA · Discovery artifact

Conversational Architecture

Organization of user intents, dialogue structure and proposed flow guidelines for the assistant.

Document status: Retrospective specification based on the available case materials. It describes intended behavior, not a deployed workflow. Language: English.

01 · Intent structure

The interaction model separates requests into three groups so that different questions do not receive the same treatment.

Normative queries

Questions about institutional guidance, rules and guidelines. Answers should be tied to approved official content and must not introduce interpretations unsupported by the source.

Procedural support

Questions about how to perform internal procedures. Guidance should be organized into clear steps and remain consistent with the available institutional guidelines.

Ticket routing

Requests that require action by a responsible team or service channel. The conversation should help the employee recognize when a question is no longer an informational query.

02 · Main interaction flow

  1. Receive the question. The employee describes the issue in natural language without having to know the correct category in advance.
  2. Classify the request. Identify whether it is normative, procedural or related to ticket routing.
  3. Clarify the context. If intent is ambiguous, ask a focused follow-up question or present objective options.
  4. Consult the source. Use only approved official content and manuals as the basis for guidance.
  5. Answer or route. Provide a clear answer when evidence is sufficient, or direct the employee to the appropriate human channel.

03 · Exception handling

The architecture defines a recovery path for situations in which the conversation cannot safely proceed.

SituationExpected behaviorExperience objective
Intent not recognizedDo not end the conversation silently. Ask the employee to rephrase or offer menu options that help classify the question.Reduce friction and recover the request context.
Ambiguous questionRequest additional information before selecting a guidance path.Avoid answers based on premature interpretation.
Official source missing or insufficientState the limitation and route the employee to the responsible human channel.Preserve reliability instead of filling gaps with assumptions.
Request outside the automated scopeDirect the employee to human support or the responsible channel.Maintain service continuity without pretending the request was resolved.

Central decision rule: institutional guidance should be provided only when sufficient official content supports it. If the source, intent or context is insufficient, the proposed flow calls for clarification or handoff, not improvisation.

04 · Conversation governance

Reference content

Approved official manuals and regulations are the permitted basis for institutional answers.

Assistant behavior

Identify intent, communicate clearly, recognize uncertainty and avoid inventing rules or procedures.

Automation boundary

When a question requires analysis or exceeds the available content, preserve the human-support route.

Evidence note: this document details the conversational behavior specified for the proposed solution. It does not prove implementation of the flows, a deployed NLP tool or classifier, technical integration, or operation in production.
