This is a reconstructed reference workflow for organizing the relationship between customer service, user guidance and routing of needs. It does not prove that a formal workflow, system integration or historical handoff measurement was implemented.

02 · Purpose and context

Purpose

Map the path followed by each request across the ecosystem, making responsibility transitions (handoffs) explicit. The model was reconstructed to illustrate how waiting points and unclear routing could be analyzed.

03 · Scope

What it covers

  • Ticket journey from initial Level 1 intake.
  • Escalation to specialized Level 2 or Level 3 teams.
  • Closure and satisfaction check.

Out of scope

  • External financial billing workflows.
  • Internal human-resources management.
  • Long-term strategic planning.
