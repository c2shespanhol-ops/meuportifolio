03 · Evidence boundary

What can and cannot be concluded

The case reports 27% fewer recurring requests and a 31% reduction in average resolution time. These outcomes are reported in the main case. This retrospective document does not add unsupported historical metrics, nor does it claim that the protocol itself was formally adopted at the time.
