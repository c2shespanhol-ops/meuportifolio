This reference map organizes the audiences and usage contexts identified for partner clinics. It is not a record of formal journey research, a satisfaction survey or measured CSAT/NPS results.

02 · Purpose and context

Purpose

Bring together stages, touchpoints, friction and opportunities in the experience of clinic professionals and managers using the Habitat platform. The source's earlier description of in-depth interviews and behavioral analysis is not supported as validated evidence in the current document; the reference has therefore been framed as a structured model rather than a formal research output.

03 · Scope

What it covers

  • Initial discovery of the solution by the clinic.
  • Onboarding and everyday use.
  • Interactions with support and customer-service channels.

Out of scope

  • Internal software development processes.
  • Habitat's corporate administrative management.
  • External network infrastructure suppliers.

06 · Evidence boundary

Interpretation limits
