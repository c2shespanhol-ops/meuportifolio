CASE 02 · PRODUCT GOVERNANCE

Product Governance for Operational Lead-Time Reduction

I organized an uncontrolled request flow, restored user visibility and used process evidence to reduce delays and rework.

RESULT71% reduction in lead time, with requests resolved in up to 48 hours.
MY CONTRIBUTIONJira, Kanban, prioritization, SLA monitoring, IT interface and user validation.
01 · APPROACH

From intake to validation.

INTAKE

Standardize the request

Structured request information in Jira to reduce ambiguity and rework at the source.

PRIORITIZATION

Decide by impact

Analyzed requests by operational impact and urgency instead of simply processing them by arrival order.

FLOW

Make the work visible

Tracked requests through execution, SLA references, exceptions and technical dependencies using a Kanban flow.

VALIDATION

Close the loop

The ticket was not considered complete just because IT responded. The user tested the solution and confirmed the problem was resolved.

02 · EVIDENCE

What changed.

Less ambiguity

Structured intake and clearer information reduced interpretation and rework.

Visible exceptions

Technical visits and overdue requests became visible in the flow and could be escalated with evidence.

User control

Delivery was followed through user testing instead of ending with a technical response.

Measured impact

Jira charts and delivery conversations supported the reported 71% lead-time reduction.

Ownership boundary
The experience demonstrates product practice and flow governance, but is not presented as formal end-to-end Product Owner ownership.
