Product Governance for Operational Lead Time Reduction

ROLE & OWNERSHIP

What I did and what this case proves.

My contribution: I structured the operational flow in Jira and Kanban, supported prioritization and SLA monitoring, acted as an interface with Technology and validated delivery with users. Limit: this demonstrates Product practice and flow governance, not formal end-to-end Product Owner ownership.

01 · THE PROBLEM

The problem was not only delay.

Requests entered without control

Users and stakeholders often came directly for help. Requests could lack protocol, deadline, owner or clear tracking, and some were not completed.

Users lost control

The flow was slow as well as opaque. The challenge was to establish control and identify where time was being lost.

02 · HOW I ACTED

First I organized the flow. Then I looked for speed.

Understand

Talked with users and stakeholders and observed recurring service problems.

Research

Evaluated alternatives to Trello and selected Jira for more dynamic ticket control.

Organize

Structured a Kanban flow with owners, categories, priorities, statuses and deadlines.

Prioritize

Impact and user value guided operational prioritization rather than simple arrival order.

Monitor

Used the IT Service Charter as a deadline reference and created an internal 24-hour monitoring margin.

Validate

A ticket was not considered complete just because Technology responded. The user tested the solution and confirmed resolution.

03 · WHAT CHANGED

Once the flow became visible, the bottlenecks became visible too.

Less interpretation. Less rework.

Screen captures clarified system-access requests and reduced cases where delivered access differed from what was requested.

Exceptions became visible

Requests dependent on technical visits became explicit in the flow, improving tracking of operational constraints.

04 · RESULT

71% reported Lead Time reduction

REPORTED IMPACTApproximately one week → requests reaching 48 hours

Evidence scope: the result comes from the operational flow described in this case. The experience demonstrates demand-flow management, standardized intake, operational prioritization, Jira/Kanban, traceability and user validation. It is not presented as formal product ownership.
