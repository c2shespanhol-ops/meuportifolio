CASE 02 · DOCUMENT 05 · DECISION MAKING

Decision and Escalation Playbook

English version of the original supporting document. The content preserves the scope, reported outcomes and evidence limitations of the source.

01 · Decision flow

How a request was handled

  1. 01 · Is the request clear?If not, return it for curation and guide the employee on form completion, documentation or ticket type.
  2. 02 · Is the ticket type correct?Validate incident or service request according to the Service Charter and context.
  3. 03 · Is there movement?If the ticket remains inactive beyond the monitoring point, contact TIC.
  4. 04 · Has the Service Charter deadline passed?Request an update and, when justified, review the request classification.
  5. 05 · Has the solution been validated?The employee tests the outcome. Incorrect or incomplete delivery returns for correction.
02 · Escalation triggers

When to escalate

Deadline

No response or movement after the internal monitoring period.

Impact

The request blocks an employee from performing a necessary activity.

Quality

The technical delivery does not match the request.

Security

Access permissions exceed or fall short of the employee's functional need.

03 · Ownership

Responsibility for the journey

The focal-point role established clear responsibility: no request should be left without an owner, monitoring or validation. The playbook documents this operating principle.
