This is a reference proposal for classifying and routing incidents. There is no evidence that Habitat operated with this severity matrix, formal SLAs, assigned roles or Product Backlog integration. Response and resolution times are not defined.

02 · Purpose and context

Purpose

Provide a structured reference for incident review, prioritizing and routing failures, with the intended aim of minimizing operational impact on partner clinics and maintaining traceability during resolution. The source frames the proposal as a response to needs identified in operational discovery; it does not establish historical deployment.

03 · Scope

What it covers

  • Critical availability incidents.
  • Processing errors and corrupted data.
  • Failures in integrations with external APIs.

Out of scope

  • Enhancements and new feature requests.
  • Usability questions without system blockage.
  • Contractual plan changes.

Intended outcomes listed in the source include reducing unplanned downtime, clarifying responsibilities during incidents and producing root-cause reports. These are intended outcomes, not verified results. Potential applications include organizing triage, clarifying status and next steps, structuring incident analysis and recording recurring needs for Product review.

07 · Evidence boundary

Interpretation limits

The source explicitly states that no historical SLAs, formal role assignments, Product Backlog integration, MTTR or SLA adherence results are evidenced. This document is a proposal/reference model, not proof of an implemented incident-management operation.
