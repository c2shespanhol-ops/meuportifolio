ROLE & OWNERSHIP

What I did and what this case proves.

My contribution: I structured Discovery, product vision, personas, journey, conversational architecture, tone of voice, functional requirements and the conceptual prototype. Limit: this case is not presented as evidence of formal ownership of Product Strategy, Product Backlog or product metrics.

03 · PRODUCT READING

From need to experience design.

Discovery

The work structured the need, users, journey and usage context before implementation.

Experience architecture

Conversational architecture, tone of voice, requirements and prototype translated the intended experience into tangible artifacts.

Internal approval and use

The persona was approved by management and used in internal Secretariat channels, including management presentations and meetings. This does not establish that the assistant was deployed in production.

Evidence boundary

The case demonstrates Discovery, user understanding, journey mapping, requirements and institutional solution design as solution design and specification, without claiming production deployment, formal product strategy ownership or product metrics.
