Case 01 · Tax and Fiscal Management Secretariat

Institutional virtual assistant: Discovery before technology

Structured concept for a virtual assistant to support internal services for Secretariat employees, centralizing knowledge and reducing operational dependency.

About 2 min read

What this case delivers
Product vision, personas, journey, conversational architecture, requirements and conceptual prototype
Primary user
Secretariat employees seeking communications, quick answers and internal procedures
Status
Concept and specification. Not deployed in production
Approval
Persona approved by management and used in internal channels
Context and period
SEGET, Maricá City Hall, January 2025 to May 2026

My role: I structured Discovery, product vision, personas, the user journey, conversational architecture, tone of voice, functional requirements and the conceptual prototype. Evidence boundary: this case does not demonstrate assistant deployment or formal ownership of Product Strategy, Product Backlog or product metrics. No quantitative outcome is claimed.

01 · Product perspective

From the need to experience design

Discovery

I structured the need, users, journey and usage context before any implementation, using product vision, persona and journey artifacts.

Experience architecture

The concept was detailed through conversational architecture, tone of voice, functional requirements and a conceptual prototype.

Internal approval and use

The persona was approved by management and used in internal Secretariat channels, including visual management presentations and meetings.

What this does not prove

The use of the persona does not prove that the assistant was deployed. This case demonstrates Discovery and specification.
