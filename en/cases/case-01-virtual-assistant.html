CASE 01 · INSTITUTIONAL VIRTUAL ASSISTANT

Building an Institutional Virtual Assistant

Product Discovery applied to the design of an institutional virtual assistant for internal public-sector users.

PRODUCTInstitutional virtual assistant for internal knowledge and support.
MY CONTRIBUTIONDiscovery, product vision, personas, journey, conversational architecture, requirements and conceptual prototype.
01 · APPROACH

Product before technology.

DISCOVERY

Understand the need

Structured the users, context and intended use before implementation.

EXPERIENCE

Design the interaction

Created personas, user journey, conversational architecture and institutional tone of voice.

REQUIREMENTS

Make the idea executable

Documented functional requirements and a conceptual prototype for the first version.

VALIDATION

Connect the work to adoption

The persona was approved by management and adopted in internal channels, including Gestão à Vista presentations and meetings.

02 · EVIDENCE

What this case demonstrates.

Discovery, user understanding, journey mapping, requirements, conversational architecture and institutional solution design.
