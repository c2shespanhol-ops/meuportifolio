Proto-personas

Reference profiles considered during the assistant's conception.

Document status: Retrospective documentation based on the available case materials. Language: English.

01 · Primary profile: operational employee

Context: handles frequent process questions, internal procedure doubts and requests for normative guidance at the Secretariat. Needs: quick, accurate answers without navigating extensive manuals or waiting for help with basic questions. Pain points: time lost searching fragmented information across legacy systems and outdated portals.

02 · Secondary profile: area manager

Context: oversees process compliance and aligns the team with current guidelines. Needs: ensure that the team uses reliable, standardized information sources. Pain points: communication bottlenecks and rework caused by conflicting guidance across departments.

03 · Evidence note

These are working proto-personas. Names and characteristics are illustrative. They do not represent real individuals or the results of formal user research.
