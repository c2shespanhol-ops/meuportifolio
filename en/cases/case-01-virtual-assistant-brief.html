CASE 01 · MARIA · RECONSTRUCTED BRIEF

Product brief

A reference brief reconstructed from the available artifacts and recorded professional context. It is not presented as the original approved brief or as a verbatim institutional request.

Methodological note: the original brief is unavailable. The sections below distinguish documented context, product interpretation and decisions that would need stakeholder confirmation before implementation.
01 · CONTEXT

Institutional context and problem

The work took place within the Tax and Fiscal Management Secretariat of the Municipality of Maricá. The institutional virtual assistant was conceived to help employees access internal guidance, information and procedures, reducing reliance on scattered knowledge and repeated manual inquiries.

Product problem, framed as a hypothesis

Employees may struggle to locate consistent, current and relevant institutional guidance quickly. Dispersed information can lead to repeated questions, rework and dependence on manual referrals.

02 · OUTCOME AND VALUE

Proposed objective

Structure a conversational experience that helps users consult approved institutional content, provides clear answers and routes uncovered or ambiguous cases to the appropriate human channel.

Employee value

Find guidance and procedures with less search effort.

Management value

Organize institutional knowledge and expose content gaps.

Operational value

Standardize reference answers and improve unresolved inquiry handoff.

03 · USERS AND SCOPE

04 · EXPERIENCE PRINCIPLES

Solution guidelines

  • Prioritize clarity, objectivity and accessible institutional language.
  • Restrict answers to approved official content.
  • Do not invent regulations, deadlines, procedures or interpretations.
  • State limitations when information is insufficient.
  • Offer clarification or human handoff when needed.
  • Plan for content ownership and review.

06 · OPEN QUESTIONS

What would need confirmation

  • Which topics and procedures belong in the first release?
  • Who approves and maintains each source?
  • Which channels and systems would be used?
  • What is the official human handoff process?
  • Which security, privacy, access and audit requirements apply?
  • Which targets and criteria define a successful pilot?
These are discovery gaps and should not be retrospectively filled with assumptions.
