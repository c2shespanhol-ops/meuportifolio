CASE 02 · DOCUMENT 07 · PRODUCT SUCCESS

Success Metrics and Process Health

English version of the original supporting document. The content preserves the scope, reported outcomes and evidence limitations of the source.

01 · Main outcome

Lead-time reduction

Approximately 71% reduction in lead time. The time required for requests to move through the workflow fell after intake centralization, request curation and active monitoring. The reported reference moved from around one week to generally up to 48 hours.

02 · Process maturity

Qualitative health indicators

  • Standardized process: entry, classification and validation criteria became known.
  • Defined decision criteria: classification, deadline, impact and need guided request handling.
  • Governance in place: a layer was responsible for curation, follow-up and escalation.
  • Structured communication: employees and IT had a centralized point of contact.
  • Validated experience: employees tested solutions before closure.
  • Access security: profiles considered job requirements and changes in assignment.
03 · Measurement logic

Speed without losing quality

Lead time was the main quantitative indicator, but it was not considered in isolation. Process success also depended on traceability, information quality, access security, clear responsibilities and validation of the resolution. No additional numerical results are claimed here. Qualitative indicators describe observed dimensions of process maturity.
