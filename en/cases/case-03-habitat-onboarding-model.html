This onboarding guidance model was reconstructed from the production of step-by-step materials, videos and content reused in self-service, chatbot and training. There is no validated evidence of measured Time-to-Value or completion rates; percentages were removed.

02 · Purpose and context

Purpose

Provide structured guidance for integrating, training and activating new partner clinics on the Habitat platform. The source describes the goal of helping new clinics reach initial value with understanding of essential system features.

03 · Scope

What it covers

  • Initial profile and access configuration.
  • Guided training on core tools.
  • Assisted support during the first service interactions.

Out of scope

  • Support for external third-party legacy software.
  • Custom code development for a single client.
  • Tax, legal or accounting consulting.
