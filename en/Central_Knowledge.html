OPERATIONAL DOCUMENT · HBT-KB-003

Knowledge Base

A structured knowledge repository for self-service, support guides and documentation standards at Habitat.

Code: HBT-KB-003
Version: 2.1
Status: Active & Published
Owner: CX · Produto · Operação
Reconstructed document: November 2026

Purpose

Centralize technical and operational knowledge in a single repository, reducing reliance on manual support and helping customers and employees solve recurring questions independently.

Context

Created to address fragmented documentation and make recurring questions easier to resolve through structured knowledge articles.

Scope

In scope

  • Initial clinic setup guides
  • Guides for resolving common system errors
  • Operational FAQs for support teams and customers

Out of scope

  • Engineering source-code repository
  • Confidential executive documents
  • Individual payroll records

Governance and Publication Criteria

Information Architecture: Organized around three main pillars: Onboarding Tutorials, Error Resolution and Process Manuals.

Publication Criteria: Every new article undergoes a technical review by CX · Product · Operations, a clarity review and layout approval before being added to the knowledge base.

Content Approval Workflow

Article Drafting → Technical Review → Operations Validation → Acceptance → Official Publication

Metrics and Results

Metric Purpose Result
Published Articles Cover 100% of frequently asked questions +100 articles
Reduction in Repetitive Requests Reduce basic support requests -27%

Evidence note: the article count and 27% reduction are reported case outcomes. Absolute baselines and individual adoption rates are not available in the source records.

Project Impact

  • ✓ Increased user autonomy
  • ✓ Standardized support communication
  • ✓ Reduced volume of repetitive tickets
  • ✓ Centralized, auditable documentation
