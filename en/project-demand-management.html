Honorable Mention, Servidor Destaque 2025 (Outstanding Public Servant 2025)

Technology demand management

Result: from about 1 week to up to 48 hours, tracked in Jira.

Problem

Public servants requested access, revocation, equipment and support with no protocol, deadline or owner. Some requests were never completed.

What I did

I structured the flow in Jira and Kanban, prioritized by user impact, used the IT Service Charter as the deadline reference with an internal 24-hour margin for follow-up, and started reclassifying requests as incidents when the deadline was exceeded. I standardized access requests with screenshots and only closed a ticket after the user tested it.

Result

From about 1 week (sometimes up to a month) to up to 48 hours, tracked in Jira. 31% less rework from incomplete records.

Recognition

Honorable Mention, Servidor Destaque 2025 (Outstanding Public Servant 2025), certificate published on my LinkedIn.

Note: Internal data from a public agency. Raw evidence not disclosed due to confidentiality.
