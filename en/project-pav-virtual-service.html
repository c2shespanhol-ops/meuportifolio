Virtual Service Points (PAV)

Result: 3 units implemented, in Centro, Itaipuaçu and São José.

Challenge

To resolve certain pending issues, Maricá residents had to go to Federal Revenue offices outside the city.

What I did

I took part in Discovery, journey mapping, alignment with the Federal Revenue Service and requirements definition (physical structure, equipment, system access, training and operation). I visited an existing PAV for benchmarking and followed the implementation, user validation and later support.

Result

3 units implemented, in the Integrated Municipal Services of Centro, Itaipuaçu and São José. Positive qualitative recognition reported by a Federal Revenue auditor.

Limit

The PAV model already existed at the Federal Revenue Service. My contribution was local, not the independent creation of the service.

Public evidence

Implementation announcement, official PAV Maricá page and official documentation of the model.
