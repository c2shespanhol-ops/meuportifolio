Privacy and transparency

Privacy policy

This page explains what information may be used when you visit this portfolio and how you can control that use.

What data may be collected

Only after you give permission, Google Analytics may record browsing metrics such as pages viewed, interactions, visit source, browser type, device, operating system, language and approximate location. These details are used in statistical reports to understand how the site is used.

Counter.dev is configured as an alternative provider. If enabled, it may process aggregated statistics about visit times and pages, browser, device, operating system, language, approximate country and referral source. According to its documentation, the service does not use cookies or IP address fingerprinting.

Resume and contact details

The resume PDF files made available through this portfolio contain contact details, including a phone number and email address. These details appear in the document for recruiters and other visitors who choose to download it.

The floating WhatsApp button contains a wa.me link. When a visitor selects it, WhatsApp opens a conversation and the visitor decides whether to send a message. The portfolio does not send a message automatically.

Purpose and sharing

The purpose is to understand the reach and use of this portfolio, identify relevant pages and guide content and navigation improvements. When Google Analytics is active, data is processed by Google LLC under Google's terms and privacy policy. Counter.dev receives data only if it is enabled as the analytics provider.

Retention

In Google Analytics 4, retention for user-level and event data is 2 months by default and can be configured for up to 14 months. The current administrative setting for this property is not verified on this page.

Counter.dev states that short-term aggregated metrics are retained for limited periods and that its daily historical archive may be retained permanently. See the provider's policy for current details.

How to refuse or change your choice

You can choose Accept or Reject in the notice displayed at the bottom of the page. If you reject, or until you make a choice, analytics scripts will not load. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage when available. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings to see the notice again.
