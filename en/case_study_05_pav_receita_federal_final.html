CASE 05 · FEDERAL REVENUE SERVICE · MARICÁ

Bringing the service closer to citizens without creating a new path from scratch.

Participation in Discovery, requirements, alignment with Brazil's Federal Revenue Service and implementation of Virtual Service Points across three Maricá locations.

Context
Municipality of Maricá · SEGET
Scope
Discovery · Service Design · Product Ops
Stakeholders
Citizens · Municipality · Federal Revenue
Scale
3 locations

The problem

Distance to service

For certain issues, Maricá citizens had to reach Federal Revenue locations outside the municipality, making in-person service more difficult and costly.

A local opportunity

The municipality could bring part of these services closer to residents through the Federal Revenue Virtual Service Point model.

Not a service created from zero

The Federal Revenue already had an established PAV model and service structure. The challenge was understanding the local problem and implementing the existing model.

Cross-organization dependency

The solution required alignment between the municipality and Federal Revenue, including physical, technology, operational and service requirements.

Discovery

01 · UNDERSTAND

Understand the citizen problem

I contributed to conversations about the need to bring service closer to citizens and the difficulties involved in accessing Federal Revenue services.

02 · JOURNEY

Map the citizen journey

The journey was analyzed to understand the need, the steps involved and where local service could reduce friction.

03 · ALIGN

Work with the Federal Revenue

I participated in conversations to understand the existing model, implementation conditions and how the operation could work in Maricá.

04 · REQUIREMENTS

Translate needs into implementation requirements

I contributed to requirements covering physical structure, equipment, system access, training and operation.

Important boundary: I did not define or prioritize the Federal Revenue service catalog, nor did I define the solution alone. The service model and structure already existed.

From benchmark to implementation

Existing benchmark

I visited an existing PAV to observe equipment, layout, space organization, service types, service execution, support, systems access and training.

Local adaptation

The visit informed the implementation in Maricá while respecting the Federal Revenue model and local conditions.

Three locations

Implementation covered the Centro, Itaipuaçu and São José municipal service units.

Follow-up

I participated in delivery follow-up, user validation and subsequent operational support when needed.
