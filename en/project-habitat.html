From one-to-one support to self-service

Reported result: 27% fewer recurring requests and 31% shorter average resolution time.

Problem

Support through WhatsApp, with no tickets or knowledge base, and recurring questions consuming the operation.

What I did

Discovery based on support conversations, prioritization by recurrence and impact, and more than 100 materials by persona (doctors, room owners and clinic owners), which also fed the chatbot and training.

Result

27% fewer recurring requests and 31% shorter average resolution time, with no absolute baselines available.

Limit

The final decision on product direction and feature priority belonged to the company owner. I do not claim formal Product Backlog or Product Strategy ownership.

Note: Both figures are reported reductions, with no absolute baselines available, and the case does not attribute the result to a single cause.
